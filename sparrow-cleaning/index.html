FAQ

Questions, answered

Are you insured, and are cleaners background-checked?

Yes to both. Sparrow Cleaning Co. is fully insured and bonded, and every cleaner passes a background check and reference verification before joining our team.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

Not at all. Most clients give us entry instructions — a lockbox code, garage code, or key — and come home to a spotless house. You’re welcome to be there if you prefer.

What if I’m not happy with the clean?

Tell us within 24 hours and we’ll come back and re-clean the area free of charge. Your satisfaction is guaranteed.

Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?

Yes — supplies, products, and equipment are all included in your price. If you’d like us to use specific products you provide, just let us know.

How do I pay?

Securely online by card. We keep a card on file and charge it after your clean is complete — no cash or checks needed.

Can I get the same cleaner each time?

For recurring plans, yes — we match you with a consistent cleaner who gets to know your home and preferences.

What’s your cancellation policy?

Plans change — we get it. Reschedule or cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before your appointment.

Which areas do you serve?

Raleigh and across Wake County — including Cary, Apex, Morrisville, Holly Springs, Fuquay-Varina, Wake Forest, Garner, Knightdale, and nearby. Ask us if you’re just outside that range.
