# Outreach-100 (built 2026-07-04, nothing sent)

108 personalized outreach emails, one HTML file each, on the cream card.
Every lead is a real business verified against a source URL; 93 have a
published email address seen on their own site, 15 fall back to a contact
form or phone (the channel column says which).

## How to send one

Open the .html file, copy everything, paste into a new message from
zach@vurmz.com. Subject and address are in index.csv (also in the file's
title tag). Or paste the body text into the admin's New email composer
with "Dress it like the site" checked.

## The plan

- 5 a day, weekday mornings. At that pace this batch is about a month of
  outreach. Order in index.csv is already strongest-hand-first: trades,
  restaurants, teams/orgs, offices, pet/medical, fitness, salons, retail.
- Mark leads.csv (status new -> contacted) after each send.
- One follow-up the following week ("any interest or should I leave you
  alone"), then stop.
- The launch-price mentions ($20 pens, $24 slate, $29 boards) are true
  through July 31. After August 1, send those drafts anyway but fix the
  price line, or ask Claude to refresh the remaining batch.

## QA that already ran

No em-dashes, no banned words, every paragraph opens with the researched
fact for that specific business, one concrete offer with a real price,
no questions. Agents dropped leads with hijacked domains, closed
locations, SEO-network template sites, and out-of-state footprints.
