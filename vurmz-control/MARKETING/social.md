## Nextdoor note
Nextdoor converts for "local guy who does X" better than any platform — but it
bans obvious self-promo in regular posts in some neighborhoods. Use the
**business page** (free) + post in the "recommendations" flow. The charger and
laptop posts (#7, #10) are the most Nextdoor-native: small, relatable,
neighborly.

## Posting rhythm (sustainable for one person)
- Every finished job: 60 seconds — photo before delivery, one honest sentence
  about the material or the process, post to GBP at minimum.
- The process detail IS the content. "Two test passes to dial in mirror power"
  outperforms "Check out this custom mirror!!" every time, and it's free —
  you already know these things.
