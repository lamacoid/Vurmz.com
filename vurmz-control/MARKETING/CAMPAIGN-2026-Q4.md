## The messages, in order of use

1. **Precision laser engraving and marking.** The category claim. It is on
   the services headline and every ad.
2. **A proof photo before it runs.** The measurable edge: factory engraving
   is 7 to 14 days and blind; this is 24 to 72 hours with a photo first.
3. **Hand-delivered, by the person who made it.** Wednesday run, free over
   $50, free at any size on an account.
4. **Happy to source, for a fee.** Name the piece; it arrives engraved. The
   item at cost, the engraving, and the errand. A deposit before I buy.
5. **Design it on the page.** The card designer: sixteen layouts, fourteen
   colours, the laser file writes itself.
6. **The laser comes to you.** Live engraving for the store and the event.

Words that never appear: premium, luxury, elevate, giveaway, promo, swag,
free quote, fast turnaround, cheap, deal.

## Channels, and what runs on each

**Google Business Profile (free, weekly).** Primary category Engraver;
secondary Corporate gift supplier and Promotional products supplier.
Service area set to the cities above with Centennial as the address.
Post one proof photo a week with the material, the item, and the city in
the caption. Add the price list as Products. Answer the two Q and A
entries: "Do you engrave items I bring in?" and "How fast?"

**Instagram and Facebook, paid.** Five ads (MARKETING/ads canvas). Target
a 12 mile radius on Centennial narrowed to the zips 80113, 80111, 80108,
80121, 80124, 80122, 80112, 80206, ages 30 to 65, interests in
Williams Sonoma, Yeti, Ridge, real estate, golf. Budget $25 a day, three
ads rotating, the sourced-piece ad and the card-designer ad first. Judge
by texts and by designs saved, not by likes.

**Nextdoor, sponsored.** One landscape unit, the "your own piece" ad, in
Cherry Hills Village, Greenwood Village, and Castle Pines. $10 a day.
Nextdoor readers buy from neighbours; the copy says Centennial and the
delivery day.

**Postcard, EDDM.** One 6 by 4.25 card, front and back (on the ads
canvas). Route: the carrier routes covering Cherry Hills Village and the
Greenwood Village estates, about 2,500 addresses, once in early November
before the corporate gifting deadline. Cost about $0.24 an address plus
print, under $900. It carries the sourced list with delivered prices and
the phone number, nothing else.

**Direct outreach, text and email.** Twenty realtors and wealth managers
in DTC and Castle Pines, by name, with one sentence and a photo of a
sourced piece delivered: "Closing gifts, engraved and brought to the
table, from $185." Ten Cherry Creek North stores with the on-site rate
card. The Colorado Restaurant Association newsletter with the knife-care
piece. The outreach-100 list already in MARKETING is the base.

**Email to the existing list.** The reopen email (MARKETING/reopen-email.md)
has not gone out. Send it this week with two lines added: the card
designer and Happy to source.

**Content, three pieces that earn links.**
1. "What laser engraving costs in Denver, with our real prices." Nobody

3. "The south Denver executive gift guide, and where to buy each piece in

**New landing pages (SEO, in this order).** /corporate-gifts with per-head

## What counts

Texts to (720) 468-0138 that name a piece, per week. Designs saved in the
admin Designs room (ordered and not). Orders and average ticket on the
shop side (the target is a shop average above $90, up from the $35
anchor). Standing accounts opened. On-site bookings. Nothing about
follower counts.

Every ad and postcard links with a UTM: utm_source, utm_medium, and
utm_campaign=fall26. The designs table records where a design came from
once the referrer is stored (a small follow-up).
