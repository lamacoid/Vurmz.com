# Outreach scripts — by segment

Written 2026-06-12. Voice rules: these are written the way you talk on the site.
Send them yourself, one at a time, personalized with the lead's name from
LEADS.md. 5 contacts a day beats 50 once a month. Track sends in the CSV.

The universal rule: **lead with their problem, show one photo, ask one small
question.** Never open with your equipment specs. Nobody cares about wattage.

---

## 1 · Trades (HVAC / plumbing / electrical) — service tags

**Text / DM (first touch):**
> Hey — Zach here, I run VURMZ, a laser shop in Centennial. I make the metal
> service tags that go on installs — your name and number, engraved so it
> doesn't peel off the furnace in year two like the sticker does. Made a batch
> for a local outfit last month. Want me to drop a free sample tag at your shop
> with your info on it?

**Email subject:** Your sticker falls off. This doesn't.

**Email body:**
> [Name] — I'm Zach, one-person laser shop in Centennial (vurmz.com/services/metal-tags).
>
> Every install you do is a referral opportunity for the next ten years, but
> only if your number is still readable on the unit. Stickers fade and peel.
> I engrave stainless and anodized aluminum tags that outlive the equipment —
> phone, license number, QR to your booking page, whatever the tag needs to carry.
>
> Packs of 10 from $30. I'll engrave one free sample with your actual info and
> drop it at your shop this week. Worth a look?
>
> Zach · (719) 257-3834 (text) · vurmz.com

**Walk-in:** bring 3 sample tags (one with THEIR company name pre-engraved if
you committed to one), hand to whoever runs the office: "No pitch — stick this
on the next water heater you install and see if it's still readable when the
sticker next to it isn't." Leave a metal business card.

**Follow-up:** one text after 5 business days. "Hey [name], Zach from the tags.
Any interest or should I leave you alone?" Then stop. The no-pressure close is
the brand.

## 2 · Restaurants / breweries — knife crews + barware

**Text / DM:**
> Hey — Zach, VURMZ laser engraving in Centennial. I spent fifteen years in
> kitchens before this, so here's the pitch: I pick up your crew's knives,
> engrave each cook's name on their blade, and have them back before next
> service. $25 a knife. No more knife fights over whose Victorinox that is.
> Want me to swing by during prep?

**Brewery variant:**
> Hey — Zach, one-man laser shop in Centennial. I engrave tap handles, flights,
> and coasters with your branding — the coating burns off powder-coated steel
> clean, never washes off. Happy to bring samples by the taproom. Worth 10 minutes?

**Walk-in (the best channel for this segment):** go at 2-4pm (between services),
ask for the chef or GM, bring the engraved cleaver photo on your phone and one
engraved coaster to leave. The kitchen credibility is the wedge — use it.

**Follow-up:** drop by once more two weeks later. Restaurants buy on the second
visit, when they remember you.

> hand-deliver in the south metro — you don't have to think about it after the
> text.
>
> Send me a name and a date as a test run for your next closing and I'll show
> you what it looks like. If your team closes a few a month, I'll set up a
> standing arrangement so it's automatic.
>
> Zach · (719) 257-3834 (text) · vurmz.com

**Why this works:** realtors buy repeatedly (every closing), they expense it,
and "engraved with the closing date" is the personalization no Amazon gift has.

**Follow-up:** one email after a week with a photo of a finished board. Photos
close this segment.

**Text / email:**

## 5 · Wedding / event venues + coordinators — referral partners, not customers

These people don't buy — they **refer**. Different goal: get into their vendor
list / preferred-partner sheet.

**Email subject:** Local engraver for your couples (favors, signage, day-of gifts)

**Email body:**
> [Name] — I'm Zach, I run VURMZ, a one-person laser engraving shop in
> Centennial. Couples ask venues for personalization vendors constantly:
> engraved favors (coasters from $2.40/guest), welcome signs, cake knives,
> party gifts for the wedding party.
>
> I'm local, I turn work around in days not weeks, and I hand-deliver to your
> venue so nothing arrives broken two days before the wedding. Could I send a
> small sample kit for your vendor table, or get added to your preferred list?
>
> Zach · (719) 257-3834 · vurmz.com

**Follow-up:** offer to engrave something for the VENUE free (a host stand
sign, table numbers) — the venue using your work daily IS the referral engine.

## Cadence that won't burn you out
- **Daily (15 min):** 5 first-touches from LEADS.md, mark the CSV.
- **Weekly:** follow-ups for anything 5+ days old, one walk-in run (batch 3-4
  restaurant/trade stops in one drive).
- **Rule:** every outreach references something specific about THEM (from the
  "why this one" column). Generic blasts read as spam and get deleted; that's
  why the lead list has that column at all.
