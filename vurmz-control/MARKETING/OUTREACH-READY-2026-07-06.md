## The thinking (read once, then just send)

**Why this batch beats the June scripts.** Three things exist now that did
not on June 12: the metal labels pack is a live, purchasable page with a
studio photo (vurmz.com/services/metal-tags), business terms are real
(10% off at 50+ units, 15% plus free delivery plus NET-30 at 150+), and
there is a folder of clean product photos on your Desktop
(vurmz-product-photos). Photos close. Every draft below attaches one.

**Timing: do not send this weekend.** July 4 lands Saturday. B2B inboxes
are empty Thursday and Friday, and anything sent now sits at the bottom of
a Monday pile. Restaurants are slammed all weekend; walking in would burn
the first impression.
- Emails: Monday July 6, 8:30 to 9:30am. First thing they read that week.
- Texts: Monday July 6, between 10am and 2pm.
- Restaurant and brewery walk-ins: Tuesday July 7 to Thursday July 9,
  2 to 4pm, between services.

**Volume: 5 a day, Monday to Wednesday.** Your own cadence rule, and it
holds. Day 1 trades, day 2 restaurants and new openings, day 3 realtors
and offices. Follow-ups start the following Monday (July 13): one message,
"any interest or should I leave you alone", then stop.

**The one photo rule.** Attach exactly one image per message. More reads
as a catalog blast. The right one per segment is named in each draft.
For trades, the strongest proof you own is the label ON YOUR OWN LASER
(vurmz-laser-plate.jpg): it says I sell this because I use it.

**Sample economics, so the free offers do not feel soft.** A sample tag
costs you about $3 in material and 10 minutes. One trades account that
reorders packs twice a year is worth $80+ annually, and they refer other
trades. The free sample is the cheapest ad you will ever run.

**Compliance and tone.** These are one-to-one introductions from a named
local person with a real address and phone, not a blast; that is fine
under CAN-SPAM. The exit is always offered ("or should I leave you
alone"), which is also the brand.

Photos referenced below live in ~/Desktop/vurmz-product-photos/ unless
marked (site) for a live URL.
