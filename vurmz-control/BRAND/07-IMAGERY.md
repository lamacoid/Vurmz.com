# VURMZ Brand Binder
## 07 · Imagery

> How VURMZ uses photos and art. Plain language, no em-dashes.

### The rule that matters most
**Real work only. Never generic stock.** Use VURMZ's own photos of real jobs. The one time the site used stock catalog images (the old materials grid), they broke and looked fake. If there is no real photo, use a clean placeholder, never a mismatched stock image.

### Photography
- Subject: the actual engraved piece, in a real setting (a hand, a bench, a kitchen, a bar). Honest, not staged-corporate.
- Hero treatment: work photos run behind a teal film and fade into the page, forming a dark banner. The photo is texture and proof, the words sit on top.
- Product shots: clean, the real product, consistent framing. Until a real photo exists, show a plain "photo coming soon" placeholder. Real product photos are still the top gap and the biggest conversion lever.
- Source of the portfolio set: `lib/portfolio.ts`.

### Engraving art and design elements
- The design library (curated, watermarked thumbnails) is generic, ownable art: bones, florals, patterns, symbols, nature, occult, clip art. No trademarked or licensed characters. No cutesy mascots.
- Customer-supplied art: SVG or PDF engraves best (crisp, high contrast). Photos and PNG/JPG are accepted and cleaned up. A proof goes out before anything is cut.
- See `vurmz-control/DESIGN-LIBRARY.md`.

### Rules
- Honest over polished. A real photo of a real job beats a perfect stock render every time.
- Keep the teal film and the fade for hero photos so they read as one banner.
- No stock people, no fake "team" shots. It is one person.
