# VURMZ Brand Binder
## 09 · Applications

> Where the brand shows up off the homepage. Plain language, no em-dashes. Everything here uses the locked palette, type, logo, and voice.

### Already in the repo
- `app/favicon.ico` and `favicon.svg`, `apple-touch-icon`, `app/manifest.ts`.
- OG image referenced in metadata (`images/og-image.jpg`).
- Admin PWA: `public/admin.webmanifest` + `scripts/make-admin-icons.mjs`.
- Marketing kits in `vurmz-control/MARKETING/` (outreach, GBP, social).

### To produce (open)

- **Social templates.** Instagram, Facebook, X, LinkedIn post and story frames in the paper-with-teal-ink system. Pull captions from the verbal layer.
- **Business card.** The obvious move: engrave a metal card. It is the product demoing itself. Anodized aluminum or stainless, the standalone RM mark, phone, "Local. Thoughtful. Fast."
- **Email signature.** Plain, the mark, name, phone, one line.
- **Google Business Profile.** Logo, cover, service list, posts. Kit notes in `vurmz-control/MARKETING/gbp`.
- **OG / favicon check.** Confirm the current OG image and favicon match the refreshed brand; redo if stale.

### Rules
- Every asset uses the real palette and the real logo, not approximations.
- Voice rules apply to all copy on these assets: concise, plain, no hype, no competitor framing, no em-dashes.
- Physical pieces should, where possible, be engraved by VURMZ. The brand sells the proof.
