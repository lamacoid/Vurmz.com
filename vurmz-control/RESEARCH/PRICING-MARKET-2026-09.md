# VURMZ pricing market study, sourcing, buyer research and SEO plan. September 2026

Prepared 2026-09-13. Every price below was read from a live page on that date ("Seen" column) unless marked "search snippet", which means the number appeared in a search result excerpt for the URL given but the page itself refused to load. Pages that refused (403, 429, expired certificate, DNS) are listed in "What I could not verify" rather than guessed. Ranges are ranges; quote-only lanes are marked quote-only.

Scope, per the owner's direction:
- The SHOP side (gifts, bring-your-own, coaster and drinkware sets, decor, keychains, pet tags, single cards and pens, cutting boards, jewelry, one-off pieces) is repriced UPWARD to a high-end niche position.
- The SERVICES side (equipment labels, knife crews, tool marking, metal card packs, pen packs, business coaster packs, installer tiles, plates and panels, volume tiers) is HELD. Each row is checked against comparables and against the money-loss floor (blank cost times three).
- Part 4 covers premium items the owner would buy in person in the south Denver metro, engrave, and hand-deliver, with concierge terms.
- Part 5 covers what affluent south-metro buyers want, and on-site live engraving.

How to read the comparables: (a) Denver-metro engravers and trophy shops, (b) national premium custom-engraving shops, (c) marketplace-style small sellers (Etsy itself blocked every fetch), (d) promo and industrial vendors as the floor.

### Concierge terms (proposed)

What the market does: every engraver that sells engraving on a product marks it final sale (Kershaw, District Cutlery, Japanese Knife Imports, Smithey). Leatherology charges a "custom production fee" for custom orders and shows a digital mockup first. Decanter Engraving charges $30 to $40 for a second engraving location and $35 for priority. Carla Schall charges $10 to $25 for presentation packaging. Nobody publishes a personal-shopping engraving service in the metro; Interaction Denver and Social Imprints treat it as quote-only white-glove work.

Proposal for the site, under "Concierge: we buy it, engrave it, and bring it to you":
1. **Quote first.** Customer names the item (or picks from the list). VURMZ replies within one business day with the item's current retail price, the engraving, the concierge fee, and a delivery day.
2. **Deposit: 100% of the item price plus 50% of the engraving and concierge fee, taken when the quote is approved and before the item is bought.** The balance is charged when the proof photo is approved. Rationale: the item is a real cash outlay on the customer's behalf; national vendors take full payment up front for custom work (Leatherology, Decanter Engraving, all the knife houses).
3. **Cancel before purchase:** full refund. **Cancel after purchase, before engraving:** VURMZ returns the item where the store allows it and refunds the item price less any restocking fee the store charges; the concierge fee is kept because the errand was made. If the store will not take it back, the customer keeps the item at cost and pays the concierge fee only. **After engraving:** final sale, like every comparable.
4. **Proof photo before every run.** One revision included.
5. **Pricing:** concierge fee $45 (item under $100), $65 ($100 to $300), 20% of item ($300+); engraving on a sourced item $45 for one location; second location +$30 (Decanter Engraving's structure). Gift box or presentation wrap +$15 (Carla Schall $10 to $25).
6. **Turnaround:** delivery within 3 business days of deposit for items stocked in the metro, since the buying trip, the proof, and the run each take a day; online-only items add shipping time and are quoted.
7. The $25 courtesy fee is retired; it priced a personal errand for an affluent buyer below what Nordstrom charges to monogram a towel.
