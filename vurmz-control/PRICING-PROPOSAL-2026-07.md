Prepared 2026-07-03 from the sourced market analysis (per-price-point
comparables: Seton, 4imprint, Lazer Designs, MetalKards, Etsy/CraftsTrack,
PetSmart, Thumbtack Denver, Personalization Mall; full table with sources
in the session record). Doctrine from MASTERPLAN-2026-07.md section C:
silly is surgical, fight on structure, cut only where a competitor posts a
number we can visibly halve, protect the premium shelf and Q4.

## HOLD (protect the brand and Q4)

- BYO $35 flat: it is already the cheapest posted number in the metro
  (Thumbtack floor $45) AND it gatekeeps the calendar from $10 trinket
  trips. The analysis says $25 is safe; the risk table says it invites
  the wrong customer. Hold.
- Boards from $45, stainless coaster sets $44/$58, photo panel $75,
  slate set $34, tool marking, sourcing $25: premium shelf and
  uncontested lanes. Cutting signals down and burns real material margin.
- Consumer catalog broadly: it is July; these are the Q4 prices. Revisit
  after the season, not before it.

## FRAMING (zero price change, the loudest moves)

1. "The engraving is free." On every catalog item: price the object,
   personalization $0. Same revenue, unmatchable line (their setup fee
   forbids saying it).
2. The structure strip on services pages: "No setup fee. No minimums.
   No shipping. Proof before it runs. Delivered in days."
3. Per-unit lines in tier cards: "$2 a tag at 100."
4. "Next-day is the default, not a rush fee."

## Rollout (after sign-off)

One sweep, the 0010-0013 pattern: D1 backup, lib/pricing.ts constants,
D1 price updates + 2 new SKUs (keychain single, pet tag), tier copy on
/services, PRICING.md + PLAYBOOK.md, framing copy, verify quotes
byte-match for held prices, deploy, push. Analytics events land FIRST so
the change is measurable (masterplan G).

## Expected complaint from a competitor reading the site

"They post $2 a tag delivered with no minimum and free engraving; we
charge a $25 setup fee before the first tag comes off the machine."
That is the reaction the whole sheet is engineered for.
