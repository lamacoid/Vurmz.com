# Metal card templates (2026-07-17)

The eight named layouts customers can pick on the metal card products
(wallet card, business pack of 10). The pick rides the order as
options.template and shows on the ticket as "Layout: <name>". This file
is the production spec: when a ticket names a layout, build the proof
from the recipe here so the same name always means the same card.

Grounded in a 100+ card reference sweep (Pure Metal Cards, MMBC,
RockDesign, Etsy engraved-aluminum sellers, design-blog taxonomies),
July 2026. House rules that apply to every template:

- One tone. Hierarchy comes from size only: 3x or more between the
  biggest element and the contact text.
- 4 zones max on a card. One alignment axis. One device per card.
- Contact text floor: 6 pt sans, 10 pt for serifs (thin strokes break).
  Name 10 to 12 pt, company/title 8 to 10 pt.
- Margins: keep text 0.2 in off every edge (card is 3.375 x 2.125 in).
- QR codes: 0.8 in minimum with a quiet zone, short URL only.
- Fonts come from the font book; note the customer's font pick applies
  to the NAME element unless they say otherwise.

## The eight

1. **The Crest**. Everything centered on the vertical axis: emblem or
   circled initials up top (~0.35 in), company letterspaced small caps,
   short hairline rule, name (serif, largest text), title, one contact
   line at the foot. Works with no logo (initials in a thin circle).

2. **The Divider**. Vertical hairline splits the card roughly 40/60.
   Left chamber centered: monogram or logo over stacked company name.
   Right chamber left-aligned: name, title, then up to three contact
   rows. No icons on the contact rows.

3. **The Letterhead**. Everything flush left on one margin. Company in
   letterspaced small caps with a short rule under it, big gap, name
   (serif) with title beneath, contact lines at the bottom. The most
   professional-services read of the set.

4. **The Monogram**. One giant initial (serif, roughly 1.4 in tall,
   can crop off the top or left edge) on the left; small right-aligned
   block in the lower right: name, title, two contact lines. Biggest
   size contrast of the eight; the big fill shows off the silver.

5. **The Frame**. Double hairline border inset ~0.125 in from the edge
   (outer rule heavier, inner fine). Centered stack inside: company,
   name, title, contact. No emblem. The fine engraved lines are the
   device; laser holds lines print cannot.

6. **The Band**. Etched dot or texture band down the left edge
   (~0.4 in wide, engraved at low power so it reads dimmer than the
   text). Remaining area flush left: company small caps, name, title,
   contacts. The band can carry a subtle pattern from the design
   library.

7. **The Scan**. Left block: company small caps, name, title, phone.
   Right block: QR code at 0.8 in with the URL in text underneath.
   The QR must be generated from a short URL and test-scanned off the
   actual material before delivery (silver on dark anodized scans
   well; test extra carefully on stainless).

8. **The Signature**. The customer's name set large in a script face
   from the font book, centered; title in letterspaced caps beneath;
   one contact line at the foot. Their font pick matters most here.

Not offered (on the bench from the same research, if demand shows up):
full-bleed pattern with clear window, oversized wordmark, vertical
orientation, illustrated footer, credit-card pastiche, corner grid.
